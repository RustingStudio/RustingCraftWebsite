M7 is the craft tier: the step from stone tools to metal ones.

## What works now

- **Furnaces.** Craft a smelting recipe next to a furnace and the server
  queues the batches and burns the fuel. Use the furnace to see fuel time and
  progress and to take the output. Smithing XP goes to whoever loaded the ore.
- **Copper and bronze.** Tools, weapons and armour, gated by Smithing. Bronze
  needs Smithing 5: 3 copper ingots and 1 tin ingot make 3 bronze.
- **Quality.** Crafted weapons and armour roll a quality from Crude to
  Masterwork, which scales damage and armour points. The tooltip shows the
  quality, the stats and who made it.
- **Chests.** 27 slots, shared between players. Breaking a chest spills its
  items.
- **Doors.** Two blocks tall, opened with Use.
- **Homes.** A closed room with a door, a bed and a hearth is a home.
  Sleeping there gives Building XP and Rested for 10 minutes plus 1 minute per
  comfort point, with faster health regeneration.

The suite stood at 284 tests when M7 closed.

## What still looks rough

The furnace has no glow, fire or smoke while it burns. Chests and doors snap
open with no animation or sound. Coal and copper icons both read "Co".
